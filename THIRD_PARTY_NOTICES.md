# Third-party libraries

The app vendors browser distributions so that document parsing works without a CDN or an installation step.

- **PDF.js 5.6.205** — Mozilla and contributors, Apache-2.0. Source: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js . License: `public/vendor/pdfjs/LICENSE`.
- **JSZip 3.10.1** — Stuart Knightley and contributors, dual MIT / GPLv3. This project uses the MIT license. Source: https://github.com/Stuk/jszip . License: `public/vendor/jszip/LICENSE.markdown`.

Original library notices remain in the vendored files. PDF.js CMaps, standard fonts, and WASM dependencies include their original licenses.
